Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

StepRally is built for private step challenges. We do not sell your personal information, use health data for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StepRally ("StepRally," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the StepRally mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

We collect your email address and the profile information you choose to provide, such as your display name, username, avatar emoji, time zone, and daily step goal.

Health and fitness information

With your permission, StepRally reads step-count information from Apple HealthKit. We use this information to display your progress, calculate rally standings, and provide other step-challenge features. StepRally does not write data to Apple Health.

Friends and rally activity

We process friend requests, friend relationships, blocks, rally invitations and codes, rally membership, step totals used in rallies, rankings, achievements, and rally history.

Preferences and support

We store preferences such as sound, reduced motion, notification preferences, and time zone. If you report a problem, we collect the category, description, optional rally identifier, reference number, and information needed to investigate the report.

Technical information

Our service providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the Service, such as device or app version, request timestamps, network information, and error or security logs. We do not use this information for cross-app advertising.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Authenticate accounts and maintain profiles.
  • Sync steps and calculate goals, rankings, and rally results.
  • Enable friends, invitations, private rallies, blocks, and account preferences.
  • Respond to support requests and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Health data protections

HealthKit data is used only to provide health and fitness functionality requested by you. We do not sell HealthKit data, use it for advertising or marketing, or disclose it to advertising platforms or data brokers. You control Apple Health permission in iOS Settings and can revoke StepRally's access at any time. Revoking access stops future HealthKit reads but does not automatically delete step totals already synced to your StepRally account; you may delete your account as described below.

5. How we share information

We share information only as needed for the following purposes:

  • Other StepRally users: Your display name, username, avatar, relevant step totals, sync status, and rally results may be visible to friends, people who find your profile, or members of a rally you join. Rallies are private and invitation-only.
  • Service providers: We use providers such as Supabase for authentication, databases, and backend services, and Apple HealthKit for step-data access. Providers may process information only to perform services for us and are subject to their own legal and security obligations.
  • Legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or respond to valid legal requests.
  • Business changes: If StepRally is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy and applicable law.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate the Service, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect security. You can request permanent account deletion directly in the app under Profile → Delete Account. Account deletion removes your account and associated profile and rally data from active systems, subject to limited retention in backups, security records, or records required by law.

7. Your choices and rights

You may edit profile details and preferences in the app, manage Apple Health permission through iOS Settings, block or unblock users, and delete your account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise a right. We may need to verify your identity.

StepRally does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and therefore does not offer a sale or targeted-advertising opt-out.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

StepRally is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

10. International use

StepRally is operated from the United States. If you use the Service elsewhere, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. We will post the revised Policy here and update the effective date. We may provide additional notice when a change is material.

12. Contact us

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns may be sent to support@steprallyapp.com.
